The Label Is The Logo
The app icon is a miniature wine label — rules, name in serif, gold grape cluster, vintage year. Self-referential and conceptually tight: a wine journaling app whose icon looks like what it's about. Every element follows fine wine label conventions.

Feuille
Oldest Wine Symbol, Done Seriously
A five-lobed vine leaf — filled in antique gold, not a hairline. Veins pressed into it like an engraving. The leaf predates the bottle, the label, everything. It's on Greek coins and Roman arches. Used with restraint and precision, it's the most authoritative wine mark possible.
